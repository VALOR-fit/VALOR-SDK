
* PREAMBLE

The VALOR Group has been developing a well-established T2K oscillation analysis 
since 2010 and it contributed to nearly all published T2K oscillation papers. 
The T2K analysis code produced by the VALOR Group has evolved into a general 
oscillation analysis and framework which, amongst other activities, supports 
a) physics  exploitation in the Fermilab short-baseline (SBN) programme, 
b) oscillation physics-driven Near Detector design efforts for DUNE, and 
c) efforts for the development of global LBL oscillation fits. 

Considerable resources were invested in the development of the VALOR analysis 
procedures. The VALOR Fit is a technology that takes centre stage in the near and 
long-term physics exploitation plans of several VALOR Group members. This license 
lays a framework that maintains the exclusivity of the VALOR Group in the 
exploitation of the VALO IP, but provides the T2K Collaboration with sufficient 
access to the VALOR Fit and enables independent cross-checks of the VALOR analysis 
procedures.
